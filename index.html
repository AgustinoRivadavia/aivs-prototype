AIVS

Artificial Intelligence Verification System

AIVS is a voluntary system designed to identify and verify artificial intelligences with human-like representation.

  • Provides a unique, public identity for AIs
  • Prevents identity duplication and impersonation
  • Generates a visual verification credential
Disclaimer:
SIVS does not provide legal identity.
SIVS does not verify humans.
SIVS is a private and independent system.
