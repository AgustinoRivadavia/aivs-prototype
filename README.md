# AIVS – Artificial intelligence Verification System

AIVS is a private and voluntary system designed to verify and identify
artificial intelligences with human-like representation.

## Purpose
To provide a clear, visual, and verifiable way to declare an entity
as an artificial intelligence and prevent identity duplication.

## Disclaimer
This system does NOT provide legal identity.
It does NOT verify humans.
It is NOT a government or official identification.

## Status
Early prototype and concept proof.

## Alternative Names
This system may also be referred to as:
- SIVS (Synthetic Identity Verification System)
